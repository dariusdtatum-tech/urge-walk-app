# Daily line sources

30 quotes and 30 poem excerpts, chosen for perseverance, dedication, discipline and rising from the bottom. Every line shows its author.

Every line ships in the app (src/content/daily-lines.json) and works offline. All are US public domain: published before 1930 (works and, for translations, the translation itself). Lines were tied to the named work; widely shared paraphrases and misattributions (e.g. Emerson "What lies behind us", Thoreau "Go confidently...") were left out on purpose.

Verse lines are the existing KJV/WEB set in src/content/verses.json (KJV: public domain in the US; WEB: dedicated to the public domain).
